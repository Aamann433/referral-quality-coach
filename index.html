Referral Quality Coach

Turn what you personally observed into a clearer, more useful referral.

This checks the quality of your referral evidence. It does not assess the candidate or determine whether they should progress.

1. Choose a role

Paste another role

Paste a job description, then copy its requirements into the box below. Nothing is uploaded or interpreted by AI.

Copy the relevant requirements from the description. Each line becomes a checkbox below; the app does not extract requirements automatically.

2. Describe what you observed

What work have you personally seen this person do?

Use one example. Leave these fields blank if you have no firsthand example.

3. Connect it to the role

Explain how your example relates to at least one requirement you select below.

Which of the role requirements can you personally provide evidence against?

Mark only what you can support firsthand. One requirement is enough; coverage is not scored.

Name an area you did not observe, or describe where your firsthand knowledge ends.

    How the evidence check works
    • Red: No confirmed firsthand example with both a situation and an observed action.
    • Yellow: There is a firsthand example, but the draft is missing an observed output or outcome, relationship context, a duration, a role connection and selected requirement, or the limits of what you can validate.
    • Green: All of those evidence details are present.

    These rules check whether details are supplied, not whether they are true, specific enough in substance, or correctly linked to the role. The check ignores blanks and common placeholders. It does not rate the person or count how many requirements they meet.

    Your entries stay in this browser tab. The app does not save or send job descriptions or referral text. Refreshing or closing the page clears your draft. Use sample information for this prototype. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages; its standard request logging applies.
